Quick answers

Before you buy, know your match.

Shade matching is a reference point, not a promise. Here is how to get the most useful result.

How accurate are the cross-brand matches?

They are approximate references based on tone depth and undertone. Lighting, formula, oxidation, and application can shift the final result, so test a sample along your jawline when possible.

Why does undertone matter as much as depth?

Two shades can be equally deep but pull pink, golden, or balanced on the skin. Matching both helps avoid a shade that looks too orange, grey, or flushed in natural light.

Can I use the matcher without knowing my shade?

Yes. Start with your tone depth, undertone, and skin type. The comparison view lets you browse nearby references before choosing a current product to anchor the match.

What should I do if my skin changes through the year?

Run the matcher again when your depth or undertone shifts, especially after seasonal sun exposure. Save the closest result for each season rather than forcing one shade year-round.
